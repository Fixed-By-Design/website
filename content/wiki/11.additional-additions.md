# Additional Additions

[Additional Additions](https://modrinth.com/mod/addadd) by **Dqu1J** is the biggest third-party addition in the pack: 88 items, 17 blocks and one entity, all built to look like they could have shipped with the game.

::warning
This mod is not made by Fixed by Design. It is licensed LGPL-3.0 and its credit, licensing and support belong to Dqu1J and its contributors. This page documents it because the pack ships it, not because the project wrote it.
::

Every feature below is on. The pack ships no configuration for this mod, so the values here are the mod's own defaults.

## Two features that change how you build

Most of this mod is items. Two entries are not, and they are the ones worth knowing before you plan anything.

### Copper Patina is a second redstone wire

Scrape oxidised copper with an axe and it now drops **Copper Patina**. Placed, it carries a redstone signal exactly like redstone dust — except patina and dust **ignore each other completely**. Neither reads the other's signal.

That means two independent lines can run side by side, or cross, without connecting. No towers, no repeater tricks, no bridging blocks.

### The Tinted Redstone Lamp stops monsters spawning

The lamp registers itself as a point of interest, and hostile mobs cannot spawn near it. By default the radius is **14 blocks** and the suppression is **total** — not a reduced chance, a hard stop.

It covers natural spawns, spawns rolled during chunk generation, and pillager patrols. It does not despawn anything already alive.

## Getting around

**Rope** is a climbable block that can be waterlogged. The **Rope Arrow** is the interesting half: shoot it at a ceiling or an overhang and it lays rope downward from where it hit, so you can drop into a ravine and climb back out without building a thing.

## Tools

| Item | What it does |
| :-- | :-- |
| **Wrench** | Right-click a placed block to rotate it. Cycles whichever direction property the block has, so it works on hoppers, pistons, logs, stairs and observers alike. 256 uses. |
| **Watering Can** | Holds ten uses of water, refilled at any water source. Each use has a 25% chance to advance a crop, like bone meal but slower and free. Does not work on grass blocks. |
| **Glow Stick** | A placeable light source at level 12, two below a torch. |
| **Barometer** | Shows your Y coordinate above the hotbar while you hold it. |
| **Trident Shard** | Makes tridents craftable at last: three shards and two prismarine shards. |

## Rose Gold

A full material tier sitting between iron and diamond. The ingot is **four gold and four copper**, shapeless. Armour is made by transmuting the iron piece with an ingot rather than crafting from scratch.

| | Protection | Durability |
| :-- | --: | --: |
| Helmet | 2 | 312 |
| Chestplate | 7 | 384 |
| Leggings | 6 | 360 |
| Boots | 2 | 264 |
| Horse armour | 9 | — |

The sword deals 7 damage at 1.6 attack speed. The spear deals 3.5 at 1.0 but carries the kinetic and piercing weapon components, so it rewards momentum rather than raw swings. Tools have 900 durability and mine at speed 8.0, with the axe hitting for 9.

The material's real selling point is its **enchantment value of 17**. Gold sits at 22 and iron at 10, so rose gold enchants far better than iron while lasting roughly as long as diamond. It cannot mine anything iron cannot.

A rose gold block works as a beacon base, and there is a Nautilus armour variant.

## Music

**Albums** come in sixteen colours and hold up to **eight discs** each. Drop one in a jukebox and it plays through its contents.

The **Pocket Jukebox** does the same without a block: slot a disc into it and the music follows you until you take it out.

The mod also adds **three original discs** composed by adoghr, found in dungeons and woodland mansions.

## Suspicious Dyes

Sixteen dyes that recolour an item's **enchantment glint** instead of the item itself. Purely cosmetic, and the only way in the pack to tell two identical enchanted swords apart at a glance.

Some colours are naturally dimmer, so the mod brightens them individually — brown is boosted three and a half times, red and orange twice.

## Plants

The sniffer digs up **eleven new plant families** alongside torchflower and pitcher pods: Cottonshiver, Crimson Blossom, Amber Blossom, Bulbus, Sawtooth Fern, Frostleaf, Wisteria, Spikeblossom, Snapdragon, Lotus Lily and Mudflower.

## Food

Honeyed Apple restores 6 hunger with full saturation. Chicken Nugget restores 6 with less. Fried Egg and Sweet Berry Pie round it out.

## Where to find things

Most of this is craftable, but the mod also injects into twenty loot tables:

- **Dungeons, mineshafts and strongholds** — Barometer, Glow Stick, Rope
- **Dungeons and woodland mansions** — the three music discs
- **Creepers and zombies** — Chicken Nugget
- **Elder guardians** — Trident Shard
- **Shipwreck supply chests** — a spyglass
- **Sniffer digging** — the eleven plants

## Elsewhere in the game

Two changes are easy to miss because they have no item attached.

Brewing stands now accept **any ingredient a datapack defines**, not just the vanilla list. None of the first-party mods add a brewing recipe, so this changes nothing on its own — it is groundwork the ingredient slot needs before anything can use it.

The mod also adds **nine advancements** of its own, including one for finding a Chicken Nugget in the wild.

# Bundled mods

Fixed by Design designs four mods. The pack ships eighty-nine.

Most of the rest are performance, rendering and audio, and you will never think about them. This page is about the other kind: mods by other people that add or change something you can feel while playing. Several of them solve a problem that was on the Fixed by Design roadmap, and solve it well enough that building a version of it would have been a worse use of time than shipping theirs.

::warning
Everything on this page is made by someone else. Fixed by Design bundles and configures these mods; it did not write them. Each one keeps its own licence, its own author and its own support channel.
::

Everything below was read out of the jars and the pack's own config files, so what you see described is what the pack actually runs, not what the mod is capable of.

## Combat

**[Old Combat Mod](https://modrinth.com/mod/old-combat-mod)** by The_Hangel removes the 1.9 attack cooldown. It sets the player's attack speed attribute to a value high enough that the cooldown never applies, and it stops knockback from cancelling your sprint. It runs off a gamerule, `old_combat`, which is on by default and can be switched off per world.

**[Cut Through](https://modrinth.com/mod/cut-through)** by Fuzs makes your attacks ignore blocks you cannot collide with. Swing at a mob standing in tall grass and you hit the mob instead of clearing the grass. The pack also has its `target_alive_only` option on, so an entity playing its death animation no longer soaks up hits meant for the one behind it.

## Horses

**[Horseman](https://modrinth.com/mod/horseman)** by mortuusars is the reason mounted travel is worth doing in this pack, and it is doing far more than the pack's own mods do for horses. What is enabled:

| | |
| :-- | :-- |
| Step height | +0.1, and faster stepping down, so ledges and stairs stop killing your momentum |
| Airborne speed | Raised halfway toward ground speed, so running off a block no longer stalls you |
| Hitching | Right-click a fence while mounted to leash the horse to it; a lead slot is added to the horse's inventory |
| Summoning | A Copper Horn bound to a horse calls it from up to 32 blocks on foot, further by teleport, across dimensions |
| Free camera | Look around freely while stationary until you pass 100 degrees |
| Mining | Full block-breaking speed while mounted |
| Leaves | The lowest leaf block of a tree has no collision while riding, so forests are passable |
| Boats | Horses, donkeys, mules and llamas fit in boats |
| Chests | Shear a chested donkey, mule or llama to take the chest back with its contents |
| Powder snow | Leather horse armor works the way leather boots do |
| Wandering | A saddled horse stays within 16 blocks of where you left it |
| Interface | Hunger, experience and level render while mounted; the jump bar only appears when you jump; the horse's head drops out of your view; the mount fades out when you look down through it |

Looking at a horse while holding food it likes shows its speed, jump and health.

## Travelling

**[NetherPortalFix](https://modrinth.com/mod/netherportalfix)** by BlayTheNinth makes portals remember where you came from. Each player carries a list of portal links, so going back through the portal you arrived at returns you to the exact portal you left, rather than to whatever portal the game decides is nearest. This is the difference between a nether hub that works and one that quietly reroutes you.

## Things you can craft

**[Additional Additions](https://modrinth.com/mod/addadd)** by Dqu1J adds 88 items and 17 blocks, all built to look like they could have shipped with the game: Rope and the Rope Arrow, a Wrench that rotates placed blocks, a Watering Can, Rose Gold as a full material tier, carryable music, dyes that recolour an enchantment glint, and eleven new sniffer plants.

Two of its entries are not items at all. Copper Patina is a second redstone wire that ignores vanilla dust, and the Tinted Redstone Lamp stops hostile mobs spawning within fourteen blocks. Both are large enough to plan around, so this mod has its own page: **[Additional Additions](/wiki/additional-additions)**.

**[Armor Stand Arms](https://modrinth.com/datapack/armor-stand-arms)** by Jodek is a datapack. Armor stands get arms when placed, and sneak-right-clicking one toggles them.

**[Visual Workbench](https://modrinth.com/mod/visual-workbench)** by Fuzs keeps what you put in a crafting table inside it, renders it on the table, and shows the result floating above. Contents turn to face whoever is closest. Old crafting tables convert the first time you use them.

## Inventories

None of this changes what you can do, only how long it takes.

- **[Better Recipe Book (Extended)](https://modrinth.com/mod/OkfpeX68)**, maintained by Avalonia_0 from marshmallow's original, adds pinning, scrolling, creative-style tabs and instant crafting to the recipe book
- **[Crafting Tweaks](https://modrinth.com/mod/crafting-tweaks)** by BlayTheNinth adds rotate, balance and clear to any crafting grid; right-clicking the result slot crafts a full stack
- **[Mouse Tweaks](https://modrinth.com/mod/mouse-tweaks)** by YaLTeR turns dragging and scrolling into inventory actions. All three tweaks are on
- **[Lightweight Inventory Sorting](https://modrinth.com/mod/lightweight-inventory-sorting)** by BorknBeans sorts a container, including horse inventories and shulker boxes
- **[InvMove](https://modrinth.com/mod/invmove)** by PieKing1215 lets you walk with a screen open. This includes the Catalogue and the Map Book
- **[Shulker Box Tooltip](https://modrinth.com/mod/shulkerboxtooltip)** by MisterPeModder shows a shulker box's contents in its tooltip

## What the game tells you

Fixed by Design adds systems that are hard to guess at, so the pack leans on information mods rather than making you read a wiki mid-game.

**[Jade](https://modrinth.com/mod/jade)** by Snownee shows what you are looking at. It runs in toggle mode, not always-on, so it stays out of the way until you ask. Enabled: harvest tool, crop growth, breaking progress, container previews, enchanting power, horse stats, mob breeding and growth, armor stands, mob effects and what a mob drops next. Barriers, text displays, interaction entities, firework rockets, lightning and effect clouds are hidden from it, so builds and map-making stay clean.

**[AppleSkin](https://modrinth.com/mod/appleskin)** by squeek502 shows saturation, exhaustion, and what a food will restore before you eat it. All overlays are on.

**[Enchantment Descriptions](https://modrinth.com/mod/enchantment-descriptions)** by Darkhax says what an enchantment does, on every enchanted item, with no key held.

**[Effect Insights](https://modrinth.com/mod/effect-insights)** by Fuzs does the same for status effects, in tooltips, the effect list and the beacon menu, including the attributes they change.

**[Pick Up Notifier](https://modrinth.com/mod/pick-up-notifier)** by Fuzs logs what you collect in the corner, experience included.

**[DeathLogPlus](https://modrinth.com/mod/deathlogplus)** by NightMirror records each death with its cause, coordinates, time and a snapshot of what you were carrying. With partial keep inventory, knowing exactly what was in the lost half matters.

## Playing together

**[Simple Voice Chat](https://modrinth.com/mod/simple-voice-chat)** by Max Henkel is proximity voice. The pack ships it configured to 48 blocks, whispering to 24, with groups enabled. Nobody is required to install it to play.

**[Emotecraft](https://modrinth.com/mod/emotecraft)** by KosmX and dima_dencep plays emotes. The pack ships twelve and turns the built-in set off: backflip, clap, crying, here, hug, lean, look around, palm, point, sit, sitting and waving.

## Bugs

**[Debugify](https://modrinth.com/mod/debugify)** by isXander fixes Minecraft bugs that Mojang has not. This build carries 99 of them and the pack runs its defaults, which is all of them except two that are off deliberately. A sample of what that covers:

- Respawning no longer resets your hotbar slot
- Right-clicking no longer silently resets your attack cooldown
- Mending no longer interrupts block breaking
- Items dropped by mobs killed by lightning no longer vanish instantly
- Group AI no longer breaks when a mob's target dies
- Cured baby zombie villagers stop riding chickens
- Wild wolves no longer show breeding hearts they cannot act on
- The Ender Dragon stops circling above its perch instead of landing

Four fixes sit in a Gameplay category that the mod itself flags as potentially unfair. They are minor: riptide interacting with depth strider, the sneak animation, dropping an item while using it, and 2x2 sapling growth.

## Not on this page

Performance, rendering, audio and interface polish are the larger half of the pack and change nothing about how the game plays. They are listed on [the modpack page](/modpack), along with the resource packs and shaders.

::note
If a mod here does something you like, install it on its own. Every one of these works outside this pack, and the authors deserve the traffic.
::
